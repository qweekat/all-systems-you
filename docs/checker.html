Checks food against your own food list, the starter lists you turned on, and the limits on your My rules page. The rules are fixed, so the same meal always gets the same answer.

What it found

Your text, marked

This checks words, not amounts. It can't tell how much sugar is in a dish or spot an ingredient a recipe leaves out. To check amounts, fiber, and glycemic load, use Build and score a day.

To make it fit

This looks at which foods are in the meal, not the amounts. Suggestions follow your food lists and My rules. For exact fiber and glycemic load, add the meal in Build and score a day.

Glycemic load is GI × available carbs (carbs minus fiber) ÷ 100. Per meal, 10 or less is low, 11 to 19 is medium, and 20 or more is high. You haven't entered a carb target, so these are general bands, not your limit.

GI values come from published international GI tables (the University of Sydney GI database and Atkinson et al. 2008). Foods marked est. have no tested value, so the number is a stand-in. Non-starchy vegetables, proteins, and fiber supplements count as near zero. Look up any food at glycemicindex.com.

Sources: every list and number in this app is cited on the Sources page.

Carb and fiber numbers are approximate, not brand specific. The rules come from your own list and the starter lists you turned on. Starter lists are drafts until a clinician has reviewed them. This is a food tool, not medical advice. Check with your care team before changing your diet.
