Food breaks: low-arginine for cold sores / HSV

  1. Lysine for Herpes Simplex Prophylaxis: A Review of the Evidence
    Mailoo VJ, Rampes S. Integrative Medicine (Encinitas) 2017;16(3):42-46. PMID 30881246 · Journal article

    Found the evidence for lysine is limited; it does not set a diet.

  2. Success of L-Lysine Therapy in Frequently Recurrent Herpes simplex Infection: Treatment and Prophylaxis
    Griffith RS, Walsh DE, Myrmel KH, Thompson RW, Behforooz A. Dermatologica 1987;175(4):183-190. doi:10.1159/000248823 · Journal article

    Small trial of lysine supplements. The arginine-lysine food idea comes from this line of research; diet alone has not been well tested.

Household sharing and food cards

  1. Food Allergies: What You Need to Know
    U.S. Food and Drug Administration · Web page
  2. Mobile Health App Developers: FTC Best Practices
    U.S. Federal Trade Commission · Guidance

    Collect and share only what's needed, and let people choose. Why a food card holds food rules only, each line is ticked by its owner, and sensitive lists start unticked.

  3. Password Storage Cheat Sheet
    OWASP Foundation · Guidance

    PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 rounds, the setting used to turn a card's passcode into its key.

  4. Recommendation for Password-Based Key Derivation (SP 800-132)
    National Institute of Standards and Technology · Standard

    PBKDF2 with a random salt.

  5. Recommendation for Key Derivation Using Pseudorandom Functions (SP 800-108r1)
    National Institute of Standards and Technology · Standard

    HMAC in counter mode, used to scramble the card.

  6. HMAC: Keyed-Hashing for Message Authentication (RFC 2104)
    Internet Engineering Task Force · Standard

    The seal that catches a wrong passcode or a changed card.
