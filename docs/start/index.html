all systems you

Your health is complicated. Managing it shouldn't be. Thanks for trying it out. Pick one way to use it below.

On your phone

iPhone or Android. Nothing to install from a store, and no computer needed.

Open the app
  1. Tap the button above. The app opens in your browser.
  2. To keep it on your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. After the first visit it works without internet.

On iPhone, use the app in one place only: in Safari, or from the home-screen icon. The two keep separate copies of your information.

On your computer, with your phone

Adds pop-up reminders on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer. Your phone connects to it at home.

Download for my computer
  1. Open the downloaded file to unzip it.
  2. Open START HERE inside the folder and follow the steps for your computer.

Your information stays with you

There are no accounts. What you type is saved only on your own phone or computer, not online. Save a backup now and then from More, then Backups.

Tell us what you think

Something confusing or broken? Something you love? Just email it. A few words is plenty.

Send feedback

You don't need a GitHub account or anything on github.com. GitHub is only where the app's files are kept.

This app is not medical advice. It helps you follow the plan you and your care team set.
